How One Knob worksQuick control without hiding the real parameters

Supported effects offer a One Knob Simple view. First choose a starting point from the Presets menu to the left of Bypass, then use the central knob to make the effect lighter or stronger. At 50%, the selected preset is reproduced exactly; the endpoints are effect-specific safe ranges, not a universal Dry/Maximum scale.

One Knob uses one simple control to adjust several internal parameters of that effect at the same time.
